Especialidades

Oral Rehabilitation
It is the dental specialty in charge of restoration, meanning it restores aesthetics, function and oral health through fixed or removable dental prosthesis or dental implants. It performs the diagnosis and treatment plan appropriate to the highly complex patient who needs to recover their oral health through the most modern techniques of rehabilitation. At the same time, it establishes a close relationship with other dental specialties, such as endodontics, periodontics and orthodontics.

Endodontics
Endodontics is the dental specialty that is responsible for resolving pain, infection or inflammation of dental origin. It consists of the removal of the dental pulp for subsequent filling and sealing of the pulp cavity with an inert material.

Orthodontics
Orthodontics specializes in correcting the location of the teeth and jaws to solve problems or alterations of an aesthetic or functional nature in our face or mouth. We must remember that with the advances in dental care today, orthodontic treatments are applicable for both children and adults.

Periodontics
The specialty of periodontics refers to a professional specialist treatment that diagnoses, prevents and heals the tissues that support our teeth. These are: gums, bone, periodontal ligament and root cementum.

Oral Surgery
Dental surgery is performed through surgical procedures from children to adults with minimally invasive operations, on an outpatient basis and with good pain control, due to new technologies.

Implantology
The dental implantologist is the specialist in charge of replacing lost teeth. In order to place these new dental pieces, in general, state-of-the-art dental implants are used, which adapt well to the patient, achieving a better bite and good esthetic results.
